    Sailing controls

    Choose a harbor to trade. Open Set sail for a planned passage, or steer your boat by hand.

    • WS Forward / reverse
    • AD Turn left / right
    • Drag to look around. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
    • Select open water to sail there.
    • Approach the glowing berth slowly and follow the docking guide.
    • Open More, then Aboard, to adjust sails, repair the boat and manage cargo.

    Your merchant house

    Your practice goods and Marks stay separate. Join shared play to trade with other merchant houses.

    Sign in
    Create a house with an invitation

    A merchant house is your trading business. You start with a boat, a workshop, 600 Marks and materials. Marks are game currency. At Trusted merchant rank, your house helps fund its home city every five minutes. It pays 10% of unreserved Marks above 600 while the city holds less than 2,500 Marks. No wallet is needed.

    Recover a merchant house

    Enter your saved recovery code to set a new password. This signs out your house on other devices.

    Password & recovery

    Changing your password signs out other devices and replaces your recovery code.

    Create a new recovery code. Your old code will stop working.

    Save your new recovery code

    Save this code somewhere private. Anyone who has it can take control of your house. It appears only once. Your old code no longer works.
