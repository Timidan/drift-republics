The new captain’s playbook

One delivery.
Then the world opens.

Start in practice. You get a boat, starter stock and a job with a real use for the cargo. You do not need a wallet, invitation or payment.

Play or continue ↗An existing session resumes. Practice stock stays separate from shared play.
  1. 01

    Find Bastion’s timber order

    Open Market, choose Bastion and find the practice order for 4 timber and 120 Marks. Choose Reserve goods from your Reedhaven warehouse.

  2. 02

    Put it aboard

    Switch Market to Reedhaven. Choose Load order cargo on that order. The timber moves from your warehouse to your boat.

  3. 03

    Tie down the cargo and set sail

    Choose Lash cargo to tie it down. Open Set sail, choose Bastion and compare time, fuel, docking fees and hull wear. Choose the passage you can afford.

  4. 04

    Arrive and collect your Marks

    Arrival delivers the order automatically when the buyer has warehouse space. Check Recent notices and your Marks. The harbor office starts building with your timber.

  5. 05

    Find the next useful job

    Open the Trade chart and look for funded demand. Check ingredients in Workshop before making anything. Plan cargo for the return journey.

Need a hand? Open Settings → Open the merchant’s handbook for instructions that follow your progress. Start with planned passages. Try manual sailing when you’re comfortable.

Built with Creditcoin · Testnet

Make a cargo rig.
Sell it to a captain.

A cargo rig gives a boat four more deck spaces. Make one, then sell it to a captain who needs more room.

The buyer pays you on Ethereum Sepolia. Creditcoin records who owns the rig and verifies that payment through Attestcoin before handing it over.

Marks buy goods in the game. Sepolia test ETH pays the seller. Test CTC covers transaction fees on Creditcoin.

Start with free practice ↗ Keep sailing and crafting while a payment is being checked.
  1. 01

    Make something worth carrying

    At your shipyard workshop, craft a cargo rig from 4 timber, 3 plates and 1 repair kit.

  2. 02

    Record your rig on Creditcoin

    In Shipyard → Your fittings, choose Record on Creditcoin. The game checks that the rig was crafted. Creditcoin records its owner.

  3. 03

    Agree a price with a buyer

    In the Creditcoin fitting market, quote to a captain with a linked wallet. Choose Sign quote and reserve fitting. The rig is held for that buyer.

  4. 04

    Get paid on Sepolia

    The buyer pays in Sepolia test ETH. Your rig stays reserved while Creditcoin verifies the payment through Attestcoin. Verified payment transfers ownership to the buyer.

  5. 05

    Put the extra room to work

    The buyer docks at the rig’s harbor and chooses Install on Creditcoin. A basic cutter grows from 12 to 16 deck spaces, with a 10% drop in sailing speed.

Creditcoin trading belongs to shared play. Join a merchant house and link a wallet to trade fittings on Creditcoin testnet. Practice uses Marks and keeps its goods separate. Sailing, production and Marks are run by the game service.

How do I join shared play?

Open House in the game. Use an invitation to create a merchant house, choose a home and workshop, and save the recovery code. Your house begins with a boat, a workshop, 600 Marks and starter materials. Practice inventory does not transfer.

Do I need a wallet?

No. Sailing, crafting and ordinary trade use Marks and game goods. You need a wallet for fitting trades on Creditcoin testnet in shared play. Linking it to your house signs a message and spends no funds. Purchases need a separate wallet approval. See how Creditcoin fits in.

What should I leave until later?

Learn deliveries before borrowing Marks, combining fittings or raiding. Reinforcing a rig consumes two ordinary rigs. A commission advance is borrowed Marks. Read the repayment terms before accepting one. Outpost goods can be raided. Read the costs before committing.
